## Overview

This project analyzes the 2024 Nigerian Federal Budget sector allocations using Python. The goal is to identify government priorities and highlight areas of underfunding.

## Key Findings

- **Security First:** Security and Defense received the largest allocation at **32.60%** of the budget.
- **Education Gap:** Education received **19.91%**, which is **12.69 percentage points less** than Security.
- **Communication Neglect:** The Communication sector received only **0.24%**—a concerning gap for a nation pushing for digital transformation.
- **Health vs. Security:** Health (12.44%) receives roughly one-third of what Security receives.

## Recommendations

1. Consider a gradual rebalancing to increase investment in **Education** and **Health**.
2. Investigate why **Communication** and **Solid Minerals** receive such minimal funding—these are critical for long-term economic diversification.

## Data Source

This analysis is based on publicly available 2024 Nigerian Federal Budget data.
